A note from the dentist

You will never be told off in this chair.

I opened this clinic because I kept meeting adults who had not seen a dentist in ten years, not because they did not care, but because somebody once made them feel ashamed. That does not happen here.

We explain before we touch. We show you the fee before we start. If you raise your hand, we stop, no questions asked. And if your five-year-old only wants to ride the chair up and down on her first visit, that is a successful first visit.

— Dr. Hafiz, for the whole clinic team
